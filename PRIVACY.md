# Tidemark privacy policy

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026

This policy is published at [https://tidemark.reddevs.io/privacy/](https://tidemark.reddevs.io/privacy/). A French version is at [https://tidemark.reddevs.io/fr/privacy/](https://tidemark.reddevs.io/fr/privacy/). The app shows the same text.

## Summary

Tidemark is a read-only viewer for the health and fitness data you keep in Health Connect. It reads that data only to show it to you on your device. Tidemark has no network access. It does not collect, store, share or sell your data.

## Data Tidemark reads

With your permission, Tidemark reads these eight types of data from Health Connect:

- Steps
- Distance
- Active calories burned
- Exercise sessions
- Sleep sessions, including sleep stages
- Heart rate
- Resting heart rate
- Weight

Tidemark also asks for the **past data** permission when your device supports it. Without it, Health Connect only lets Tidemark read data from the 30 days before you first granted access, and anything recorded after that.

Tidemark reads data when you open a screen and keeps it in memory only while the app is running. It saves no copies, keeps no cache and has no export.

Tidemark never writes, changes or deletes anything in Health Connect. It does not ask for write permissions, background access or exercise routes.

## How Tidemark uses the data

Tidemark uses the data only to display it: the dashboard tiles, the trend charts and the record explorer. The explorer also shows the details Health Connect stores with each record, such as the app that wrote it, the device and the recording method.

There are no ads, no profiling and no analysis that leaves your device. Tidemark is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

## No network access

Tidemark does not have the internet permission, so Android does not let it connect to any network. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no accounts and no servers.

Some buttons open another app, such as the Play Store page for Health Connect or the source code link in your browser. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

## What Tidemark stores on your device

Tidemark stores two settings and nothing else:

- Your unit choice: follow your language and region, metric, or imperial.
- The date and time Tidemark first saw that you had granted access. It uses this to tell you how far back your data goes when the past data permission is not available.

Neither setting contains health data. Android backup is turned off for Tidemark, so these settings are not copied to backups or to a new device.

## Other apps on your device

To show which app wrote each record, Tidemark looks up the names and icons of installed apps that work with Health Connect. Android only lets Tidemark see those apps, not your full list of apps. The lookup happens on your device.

## Sharing and selling

Tidemark does not share your data with anyone and does not sell it. It has no way to send data off your device.

## Your choices

- You choose which data types Tidemark can read in the Health Connect permission screen.
- You can change or remove access at any time in Health Connect's settings. Tidemark has a shortcut to them in its settings.
- When you remove access, Tidemark can no longer read that data.
- Uninstalling Tidemark ends all access and deletes its two settings. Your data stays in Health Connect, which you manage there.
